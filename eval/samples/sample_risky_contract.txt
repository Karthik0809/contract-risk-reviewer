MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Acme Corp ("Client") and the Contractor.

1. TERM AND RENEWAL
The initial term of this Agreement is twelve (12) months. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT
Client shall pay all undisputed invoices within sixty (60) days of receipt. Client reserves the right to change the fees and rates upon notice to the Contractor.

3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All work product, deliverables, and any intellectual property created under this Agreement shall be owned by the Client as a work made for hire. Contractor hereby assigns all right, title, and interest in such work product to the Client.

4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Contractor shall be liable for all damages arising out of or relating to this Agreement. The Contractor's liability under this Agreement is unlimited.

5. INDEMNIFICATION
Contractor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the Client from and against any and all claims, losses, damages, and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

6. NON-COMPETE
During the term and for a period of two (2) years thereafter, Contractor shall not directly or indirectly engage in or provide services to any competitor of the Client.

7. TERMINATION
Client may terminate this Agreement at any time for convenience without cause upon written notice. Contractor may terminate only for the Client's uncured material breach.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY
The confidentiality obligations set forth herein shall survive indefinitely and remain in perpetuity.

9. WARRANTIES
The services are provided "as-is" without warranty of any kind. Client disclaims all warranties, express or implied.
